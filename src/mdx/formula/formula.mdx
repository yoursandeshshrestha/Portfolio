- Collaborated with designers and stakeholders to ensure a seamless user experience.

## Key Features

- **Routine Builder:** Personalized skin/hair routines with smart filters and suggestions.
- **Global Categorization:** Products by Korean, Indian, African, Japanese philosophies.
- **Wellness Profile:** Custom dashboard for user preferences, driving recommendations.
- **Brand Showcase:** Dedicated brand pages with filterable product lists.
- **Order Management:** Real-time tracking, status updates, and returns.
- **Responsive UI/UX:** Mobile-first, luxury-brand design.

## Tech Highlights

- **Next.js** for scalable routing and SSR.
- **React** for modern, component-based UI.
- **TailwindCSS** for rapid, consistent styling.
- **Custom Magento** as backend engine for e-commerce logic.
- **Shiprocket** for shipping and logistics integration.
- **Razorpay** for payment gateway integration.
- **Deployment / CI/CD** for automated, reliable releases.

## Challenges & Solutions

- **Complex filtering:** Solved with server-side logic and efficient queries.
- **Personalization at scale:** Built dynamic, profile-driven recommendations.
- **Real-time order tracking:** Engineered backend logic for live updates.

## Results & Impact

- Achieved feature parity with top beauty e-commerce platforms.
- Scalable to thousands of users and product variants.
- Streamlined user journeys from discovery to purchase.

## What I Learned

- Deepened expertise in fullstack architecture and scalable design.
- Mastered advanced Next.js, TailwindCSS, and modern React patterns.
- Learned to balance business needs, UX, and technical complexity.
